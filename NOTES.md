**macOS** (Terminal, REAPER closed):

```
curl -fsSL https://github.com/animALavoro/Objet/releases/latest/download/install-mac.sh | bash
```

**Windows** (PowerShell, REAPER closed):

```
irm https://github.com/animALavoro/Objet/releases/latest/download/install-windows.ps1 | iex
```

Then in REAPER: *Plug-ins > VST > Clear cache/re-scan*. Guide: [English](https://github.com/animALavoro/Objet/blob/main/docs/guide.md) · [Italiano](https://github.com/animALavoro/Objet/blob/main/docs/guida.md).
